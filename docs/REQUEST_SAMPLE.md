### Request type

plan-and-implement

### Objective

Export invoices as CSV.

### Expected behavior

The invoice list offers a CSV download for the displayed invoices.

### Acceptance criteria

1. The download contains one row per displayed invoice.
2. Each row includes the invoice number and total.

### Exclusions

Changing invoice totals or adding another export format is out of scope.

### Relevant context

The product already displays an invoice list.

### Risk

low
